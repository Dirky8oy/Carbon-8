01 / Technology

Next-Generation
Thermal
Infrastructure.

Magna Therma combines advanced liquid cooling technology with modular micro data center infrastructure to dramatically reduce thermal overhead, increase compute density, and enable next-generation deployment efficiency. Engineered for industrial-scale reliability, the platform delivers precision thermal control while reducing cooling power overhead by up to 10× compared to conventional cooling systems.

01

Liquid Cooling Architecture

Dual-phase liquid cooling channels are engineered to maximize thermal transfer efficiency at the source. By placing thermodynamics at the center of system design, Magna Therma removes heat faster, supports higher compute density, and significantly reduces cooling-related energy overhead compared to traditional air-cooled and DTC infrastructure.

02

Thermal Distribution

Heat is transferred from the immersion bath through a proprietary loop thermosyphon system that passively ejects thermal energy into ambient air. Designed for operation in harsh environments ranging from −10°C to 55°C, the system delivers stable thermal performance without reliance on power-hungry conventional HVAC infrastructure.

03

Modular Integration

Magna Therma systems are designed for seamless integration into existing HPC, industrial, and edge-compute environments. Flexible modular architecture will support diverse rack configurations and deployment requirements, enabling rapid implementation with minimal operational disruption or downtime.

04

Precision Monitoring

Integrated thermal telemetry will continuously monitor system performance with targeted sensor accuracy within ±0.5°C. Operators gain access to real-time diagnostics through local control interfaces and remote monitoring tools, enabling proactive optimization, predictive maintenance, and operational visibility across deployments.

05

Industrial Durability

Designed for continuous-duty operation in demanding environments, Magna Therma infrastructure will feature sealed IP54-rated enclosures, corrosion-resistant thermal pathways, and impact-resistant construction. Engineered reliability targets mean time between failures exceeding 80,000 operational hours.

06

Secure Infrastructure Control

Designed for mission-critical deployments, Magna Therma systems will incorporate tightly controlled physical and digital access layers. Secured management interfaces, isolated infrastructure architecture, and controlled service access help protect sensitive compute environments while maintaining operational integrity and uptime.

03 / Applications

01

High-Performance Computing

AI training clusters, simulation systems, and HPC workloads generate extreme thermal density that traditional air cooling struggles to sustain. Magna Therma enables stable operation, higher rack density, and dramatically lower cooling overhead for compute-intensive environments.

02

AI & Data Center Infrastructure

Next-generation AI infrastructure demands scalable thermal management capable of supporting rapidly increasing GPU power density. Magna Therma's immersion-based architecture reduces energy waste while accelerating deployment of modular compute capacity.

03

Cryptocurrency & Blockchain Mining

High-density mining operations require continuous thermal management under relentless computational loads. Dielectric immersion cooling improves operational efficiency, reduces hardware degradation, and enables higher sustained performance with significantly lower infrastructure overhead.

04

Industrial Automation & Manufacturing

Industrial environments often expose electronics and compute systems to heat, dust, vibration, and inconsistent operating conditions. Magna Therma provides ruggedized thermal infrastructure designed for continuous-duty industrial deployment.

05

Power Electronics & Energy Systems

Inverters, grid systems, battery infrastructure, and high-power electronic assemblies generate concentrated thermal loads that require stable heat dissipation. Magna Therma supports reliable operation while minimizing cooling-related energy consumption.

06

Defense & Aerospace

Mission-critical systems require resilient cooling solutions capable of operating in harsh and unpredictable environments. Magna Therma's sealed modular architecture supports secure, high-performance compute and electronics deployment where reliability is essential.

07

Edge Computing & Modular Data Centers

As compute moves closer to the point of demand, compact and rapidly deployable infrastructure becomes critical. Magna Therma enables modular micro data centers with integrated thermal management that can be deployed in days instead of years.

08

Scientific & Research Computing

Research institutions and advanced laboratories increasingly rely on dense compute environments for modeling, simulation, and data analysis. Magna Therma supports sustained computational performance while reducing operational energy costs and thermal constraints.
