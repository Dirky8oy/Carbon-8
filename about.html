About Magna Therma

The hardest constraint in modern compute is heat.

Magna Therma is a thermal infrastructure company focused on the hardest constraint in modern compute: heat. As AI and high-performance workloads push power density past what conventional air cooling can sustain, the limiting factor is no longer the silicon. It is the power, cooling, and water required to keep it running.

We treat that constraint as an engineering problem. The HyperTherm System, our two-phase immersion cooling platform, is designed to remove heat at the source through dielectric immersion and a passive Loop Thermosyphon, with no pumps, no compressors, and no moving parts in the primary loop. Each modular tank is engineered to support up to 200 kW of compute, reduce cooling power overhead by up to 10x compared to conventional systems, and operate without water.

Magna Therma pairs advanced liquid cooling with modular micro data center infrastructure, designed to dramatically reduce thermal overhead, increase compute density, and enable faster, more efficient deployment. Engineered for industrial-scale reliability, our systems are designed to deliver precision thermal control while holding cooling overhead to a fraction of conventional infrastructure.

That architecture is purpose-built for the most demanding environments: high-performance and AI compute, industrial and edge deployments, defense and aerospace, and energy-intensive operations where conventional cooling breaks down. Wherever heat is the bottleneck, Magna Therma is engineered to remove it.

We are a serious cooling technology company, not a lifestyle brand. No gimmicks, no marketing fluff. Precise, premium, and industrial by design, built to a cleaner standard of thermal performance.

The Team

Leadership.

Co-Founder

Markus Anderson

17 Years Entrepreneurial Experience

Markus Anderson is an entrepreneur and business builder with 17 years of experience launching, scaling, buying and selling small business entities. He is currently on the Board of Directors with four startup companies ranging from construction to technology and infrastructure development firms.

Drawing from 23 years of experience working with the largest shipping and logistics company in the United States, he gained deep expertise in electrical and mechanical system integration, as well as thermal system design.

Markus's professional leadership experience spans decades in heading small businesses and holding the position of Local Chairman and National Liaison within the Transportation Communication Union BRC. He is currently the president of a tech infrastructure development company that optimizes efficiency in data center operations, specifically in the edge compute sector.

Markus excels at interpersonal relations and is leading dialogue with new potential customers and strategic partners. By establishing and building relationships across multiple industries he brings connections and fresh opportunities to new businesses early in their development cycle.

Co-Founder & Managing Member

Jake Stoker

15 Years Entrepreneurial Experience

Jake Stoker is Co-Founder and Managing Member of Magna Therma Inc., bringing 15 years of entrepreneurial experience to the company. Over that career he has helped build and grow several successful startups across a wide range of industries, from digital health platforms and physician networks to commercial general contracting and, most recently, the Carbon 8 coal export business.

He co-founded Magna Therma around a simple conviction: the bottleneck for modern compute is no longer demand but the physical infrastructure to deploy it, the power, the cooling, the water, and the time.

At Magna Therma, Jake helps drive the development and commercialization of the HyperTherm system, a modular dual-phase immersion platform engineered to dramatically reduce cooling overhead and enable compute in demanding environments where conventional data centers struggle. His focus is on translating engineering advantage into deployable infrastructure, and on building a company defined by precision, reliability, and measurable thermal performance.
